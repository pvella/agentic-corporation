## Art

All visual art in Agent Office is **original and procedural**: it is drawn at runtime with Godot's 2D drawing API in this repository's own code, and ships under the same terms as the rest of the project.

- Characters (bodies, faces, hair styles, accessories, poses, phone): `Agent.gd`
- Furniture and props (desks, monitors, chairs, coffee machine, water cooler, sofa, foosball table, meeting tables, plants, reception): `Prop.gd`
- Floors, walls, doors, corridor and room signs: `Office.gd`
- Project icon: `icon.svg`

**No third-party art, fonts, sounds or other assets are included.** Text uses Godot's built-in default font.
